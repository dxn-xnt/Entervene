## 2. Recommended Demonstration Scenarios by Account

### Scenario A: Full School Oversight & System Governance
- **Login As:** `mariano.alcantara@entervene.edu.ph` (Dr. Mariano Santos Alcantara)
- **Role:** Administrator (Principal IV)
- **What to Demonstrate:**
  1. **School Settings:** View DepEd School ID `303027`, Medellin National Science and Technology School (MNSTS), Region VII.
  2. **Academic Structure:** Active Term 2 (2026-2027 Trimester) alongside finalized historical Term 1.
  3. **Section Directory:** 6 sections across Grades 8, 9, and 10 with 100 enrolled learners.

  5. **Risk Projections:** High-level schoolwide risk distribution and prediction models.

### Scenario B: Teacher Workflow, Classwork, Grading & Exports
- **Login As:** `elena.reyes@entervene.edu.ph` (Elena Villanueva Reyes)
- **Role:** Teacher & Class Adviser (English 8, Grade 8 Diamond)
- **What to Demonstrate:**
  1. **Attendance CSV Export:** Navigate to Class Attendance, filter Term 2, click **Export CSV** to download DepEd RFC-4180 attendance logs.
  2. **Student Risk Summary CSV Export:** Navigate to Predictions, review student risk profiles, click **Export CSV** to download the comprehensive risk summary report.
  3. **Live Grading Demo:** Open the English assignment *The World Is An Apple*; open student **Jose Manalo**'s pending submission and grade it in real time.
  4. **AI Feedback Approval:** Open student **Patricia Guanzon**'s submission; view the AI-generated feedback draft, review/edit it, and click **Approve & Publish**.
  5. **Delete Feedback Demo:** Open student **Juan Aquino**'s graded submission (Grade 95.00); click **Delete Feedback**, confirm the modal, and verify the feedback is removed while the student's 95.00 grade remains intact.
  6. **Advisory & SF9 Export:** Open Advisory Class (Grade 8 Diamond); generate individual SF9 (Form 138) and batch Excel/Word reports.
